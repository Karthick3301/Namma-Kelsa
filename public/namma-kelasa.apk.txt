# Namma Kelasa Android APK File
# To serve your real generated .apk file:
# 1. Take the `app-release-signed.apk` (or `NammaKelasa.apk`) generated from your PWABuilder zip.
# 2. Rename it to `namma-kelasa.apk` and place it in the `public/` directory:
#    `public/namma-kelasa.apk`
#
# Vite, Firebase Hosting, and Vercel automatically serve files in the `public/` folder at the root URL:
# `https://<your-domain>/namma-kelasa.apk`
